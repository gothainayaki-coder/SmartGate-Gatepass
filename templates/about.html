🏛️ Institution Profile

Sri Ramakrishna College of Arts and Science for Women (SRCW) is an autonomous institution affiliated to Bharathiar University and re-accredited by NAAC. Established under the aegis of SNR Sons Trust, the college has been a premier institution championing women’s empowerment and academic distinction in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu.

The college day concludes officially at 2:00 PM. Consequently, the campus gate pass operates as a single-use exit authorization. Once a student’s exit is verified at the main security gate, the pass is recorded in institutional logs and immediately marked as COMPLETED without requiring any return workflow.

👥 Exactly Five Institutional Roles

1. Student

Self-registers into their specific degree program, submits departure requests with reason and permitted exit time, and presents the authorized digital QR pass.

2. Class Tutor

Responsible for an assigned annual class. Verifies attendance status, checks parent contact validity, and recommends genuine requests to the HOD.

3. Head of Department (HOD)

Departmental executive authority. Finalizes gate pass approvals, activates the digital QR pass, and accesses departmental audit reports.

4. Gate Guard

Stationed at the main campus gate. Employs the interactive camera scanner or manual pass code lookup to authorize student departure.

5. Administrator

Oversees user accounts, departments, courses, class tutor mappings, HOD appointments, password resets, and the complete institutional audit trail.
