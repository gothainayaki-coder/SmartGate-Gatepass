Complete Campus Architecture

Five Institutional Roles

SmartGate coordinates five synchronized campus roles to ensure student safety and transparent gate clearance.

1. Student

Submits gate pass requests with reason, destination, and parent contact details; downloads secure QR pass once fully approved.

2. Faculty / Class Tutor

Reviews pass requests for class students, confirms parent consent, validates attendance reason, and forwards recommendation to HOD.

3. HOD

Authorizes final clearance for department students; grants digital signature authorizing generation of encrypted QR gate passes.

4. Gate Guard

Scans student QR passes at college security checkpoint, logs real-time OUT time and records RETURN check-ins back to campus.

5. Administrator

Maintains institutional configuration, manages departments and users, verifies HOD registrations, and monitors campus-wide audit trails.
